## Fit and limits

Dual semantic-relation reconstruction models document, topic and word embeddings with optimal-transport regularization. Practical learned topic-model comparator, not density clustering or unknown-intent rejection. Choose topic count explicitly and budget training/preprocessing; speed and transferability in the paper are not reproduced here.

See the [ecosystem and evaluation guide](../ecosystem_2026-11-13.md) for the dated popularity snapshot and proposed comparisons. This is a reference, not a reproduction, installed dependency or implemented backend. No upstream files/weights are redistributed.
